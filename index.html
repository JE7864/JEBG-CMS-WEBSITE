Before you get started

A few useful answers.

How do I request the free trial?

Use “Request your free trial” to open the JEBG request form. The JEBG team will help you with access and setup.

Do all plans include the same core features?

Yes. All four subscription periods include full JEBG CMS access, contact and campaign management, automated workflows, engagement monitoring and the Google-powered workspace.

Can I renew my existing installation?

Yes. Existing customers can renew their subscription without changing their JEBG installation. Contact JEBG with your selected plan.

Is this website the Campaign Management System application?

This is the product website. The gallery shows actual application screenshots; the example configuration panels are illustrative. Request JEBG CMS to arrange access to the Google-powered application.
